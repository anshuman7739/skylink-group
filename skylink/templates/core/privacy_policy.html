Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

At Skylink Group of Companies (incorporating Skylink Staffing Solutions Pvt. Ltd., Skylink Skill Academy, and Skylink Maintenance Services), we respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding personal information collected via our website and application forms.

1. Information We Collect

When you complete public forms on our website (including General Enquiries, Employer Staffing Enquiries, Job Applications, Skill Academy Admissions, and Maintenance Requests), we may collect:

  • Contact details: Name, phone number, email address, city/state.
  • Employment/Candidate data: Desired trade role, experience, resume attachments, preferred location.
  • Employer data: Company name, worker headcount requirements, site location, timeline.
  • Student data: Course choices, batch preference, education background.
  • Property data: Address, service category, problem descriptions, and optional damage photos.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is processed strictly for:

  • Contacting you regarding your submitted lead or service ticket.
  • Evaluating candidate profiles against active client mandates.
  • Coordinating batch admissions, practical lab schedules, and course onboarding.
  • Dispatching certified technicians to complete requested property maintenance.
  • Generating audit logs and tracking reference numbers within our secure MySQL database.

3. Data Security & Storage

We implement industry-standard database encryption, Django CSRF protection, secure session controls, and strict role-based access for our administrators. Uploaded resumes and photos are stored in restricted media directories accessible only by authorized human resource staff.

4. Contacting Our Data Protection Officer

If you wish to review, update, or request the deletion of your submitted records, please contact us at {{ business_email }} or write to our Head Office at Skylink Tower, Turkha, Nahar Road, Gopalganj, Bihar - 841428.
