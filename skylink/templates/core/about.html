Our Foundation & Philosophy

Headquartered in Gopalganj, Bihar, Skylink Group of Companies was established to address two critical challenges: connecting talented workers and craftsmen with verified, well-paying employers worldwide, and providing reliable technical maintenance to households and businesses.

Our registered entity, Skylink Staffing Solutions Pvt. Ltd. (CIN: {{ cin }}), functions in strict adherence to statutory manpower guidelines. Together with Skylink Skill Academy and Skylink Maintenance Services, we deliver end-to-end reliability.

Our Vision

To become India's foremost trusted partner in ethical workforce mobility, technical skill enhancement, and facility management.

Our Mission

Empowering candidates with real-world skills, fulfilling global manpower requirements with integrity, and delivering quality maintenance at honest pricing.
